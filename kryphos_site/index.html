MECHANICS

How It Works

01

Trading generates fees

Creator fees accumulate as the token trades. Fees are the engine's primary funding source.

02

Fees are used first

The Boost Treasury is always consumed before any KRYPHOS from the reserve is considered.

03

Reserve covers only the gap

If funding is short, the engine calculates only the minimum reserve top-up required for the selected Boost.

04

Market guard decides

If the reserve sale would exceed the configured price-impact limit, the engine steps down or waits.
