How It Works

Creator fees accumulate as the token trades. Fees are the engine's primary funding source.

02

Fees are used first

The Promotion Treasury is always consumed before any KRYPHOS from the reserve is considered.

03

If funding is short, the engine calculates only the minimum reserve top-up required for the selected level.

04

Market guard decides

If the reserve sale exceeds the configured price-impact limit, the engine steps down or waits.
